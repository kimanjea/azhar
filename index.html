My journey

Advanced Machine Intelligence in Rapid Prototyping

Born and raised in Uganda, my journey in technology reflects a pioneering spirit and a relentless drive to build systems that matter. Today I work at the intersection of extended reality, systems interoperability, and on-device artificial intelligence — serving as Lead Interoperability Engineer at Exonicus Inc., where I lead R&D on a DoD SBIR building real-time XR medical simulation systems, and as Director of Mixed Reality and AI Outreach at AVELA - University of Washington, where I run AI * XR Labs delivering XR curricula to thousands of STEM students a year. I'm also an AI researcher for on-device AI: as Principal Investigator of AVELA's CourseLLM, I lead a team fine-tuning hardware-constrained large language models on Apple's MLX framework so educational AI can run locally, without surrendering student data to the cloud. Alongside these roles, I contribute to research across three university labs — the ISIA Lab at Université de Mons (facial expression and avatar control in XR), the Morphic Lab at the University of Washington Tacoma (reproducible gene-research tooling), and the LCI Lab at the University of Washington (justice-centered computing education) — building the research record behind my next step: a PhD in Computer Science. I got here by founding SableSync (November 2023 - June 2025) to build impactful XR solutions, developing AR experiences at Snap Inc., and teaching the first AR/VR curriculum at AVELA UW. I'm a dedicated student of leadership and human-centered engineering, and I thrive in the chaos of learning and unlearning — applying advanced machine intelligence in rapid prototyping to build things that are genuinely useful.

Story telling

RevereXR Hackathon

This project aims to leverage Steve Sneed’s experiences with The Langston Hughes Performing Arts Institute in Seattle’s Central District. Steve’s history is strongly connected to the Langston. As a teenager, Steve was part of an African dance and drumming group (the ogandes) that performed multiple times at the Langston. Steve went to college, he learned about theater direction, after that, he was hired by the City of Seattle to actually run Langston! So what he created was a cultural system, a framework that helped young people navigate challenges and define their identities.

  • Client: RevereXR Hackathon
  • Category: Immersive Story telling
